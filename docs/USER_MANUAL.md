# Tuloy User Manual

Tuloy is a hotel app. It has three kinds of users:

| Who | What they do | Where they use it |
|-----|--------------|-------------------|
| **Guest** | Book a room, request services, order food, chat, earn points | **Mobile app** (iOS / Android) |
| **Staff** | Approve bookings, check guests in and out, handle tasks, orders and chat | Mobile app **or web** |
| **Admin** | Set up the hotel, manage money, people and reports | Mobile app **or web** |

> **Guests on the web:** the web version is for Staff and Admin. A guest who signs in on the web sees "Guests, please use the Tuloy mobile app".

---

## 1. Getting started (everyone)

### 1.1 Choose your language
1. Open the app for the first time.
2. Pick **English**, **Filipino** or **Bisaya**, then tap **Continue**.
3. You can change it later in **Settings → Language**.

### 1.2 Create an account (guests)
1. On the sign-in screen tap **No account yet? Create one**.
2. Enter **Full name**, **Email or phone number** and **Password** (at least 8 characters).
3. Tap **Create account**. You land on the Guest home.

> Staff and Admin accounts are created by the hotel (Admin → Staff). They cannot sign up on their own.

### 1.3 Sign in
1. Enter your **Email or phone number** and **Password**.
2. Tap **Sign in**. The app takes you to the right area for your role.
3. Use the eye icon to **show or hide** your password.

### 1.4 Forgot your password
1. On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email or phone, then tap **Send code**.
3. A **6-digit code** is sent to you (only if the account exists).
4. Enter the code and a **New password**, then tap **Reset password**.
5. Sign in with the new password.

### 1.5 Settings and sign out
- Tap the gear icon to open **Settings**: change **Language** or **Sign out**.
- Your session stays active for a while. If it expires, you are asked to sign in again and returned to the page you were on.

## 2. Guest guide (mobile app)

### 2.1 Home
After signing in you see **Hello, <your name>** with:
- A **search card** (dates and guests) to find a room.
- **Rooms and rates**, **hotel services**, and a **map** of the hotel.
- **Getting here → Open in Maps**, and **Route to the airport**.
- A **How can we help?** chat button (concierge).
- **Profile** and **Settings** icons.

### 2.2 Book a room
1. On Home use the search card: pick **dates** and the number of **Adults** and **Children**, then tap **Find a room**.
2. Browse the results. Each room shows the price **per night**, the **total** for your nights, and "Only N left" when rooms are almost gone. If nothing fits you see "No rooms available for these dates. Try other dates."
3. Tap a room to see **Amenities**, **Policies**, check-in and check-out times.
4. Add **Special requests** if you want (optional), then tap **Reserve**.
5. The room is **held for you** (15 minutes; 30 once checkout opens). A timer shows how long is left. If it runs out you see "Your hold expired. Please search again."
6. On **Review and pay** you see the price, **Service charge**, **VAT** and **Total**.
7. Choose how to pay:
   - **Pay deposit** (part now), or
   - **Pay in full**.
8. Payment opens in your browser. Finish paying there. The app page says **Waiting for payment…** and updates by itself. Tap **Open payment page again** if you closed it.
9. When it works you see **Booking confirmed!** with your **booking code** and room. Tap **View booking** or **Back to home**.

If payment fails, the booking is released. If you were charged, it is refunded.

### 2.3 Manage your bookings
Open **My bookings**. Each booking has a status:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Awaiting payment | Room is held, not yet paid |
| Pending | Paid, waiting for the hotel to approve (if the hotel approves each booking) |
| Confirmed | You are booked |
| Checked in / Checked out | Your stay in progress / finished |
| Cancelled, Declined, Expired, No-show | Not going ahead |

Tap a booking to:
- **Pay balance** if you only paid a deposit.
- **Cancel booking**. The app tells you the refund first ("You will get ₱X back"). Tap **Cancel booking** to confirm or **Keep booking** to stay. The refund follows the hotel's cancellation policy.
- Open the **Invoice** (charges, paid, balance).

### 2.4 During your stay (My stay)
When you are checked in, open **My stay** to see:
- Your room and stay details.
- **Wi-Fi**: the network, username and password, or a QR code to scan. In portal mode, join the network and the login page opens.
- **Hotel guide** with hotel information.
- Shortcuts to **Services**, **Restaurant** and **Chat**.

### 2.5 Request a hotel service
1. Open **Services** and pick a service.
2. Fill in the form (the questions depend on the service). Examples: preferred time, notes, airport transfer details.
3. Choose how it is paid when offered: **Charge to my room** (only while checked in) or **Pay online**.
4. Tap **Send request**.
5. Track it in **My requests**. A **status timeline** shows each step.
6. Some services work by quote: the hotel sends a price, and you tap **Accept quote** to go ahead.
7. You can **Cancel request** while it is still allowed.
8. **Airport transfers** show a map and a route to the airport.

### 2.6 Order food (Restaurant)
1. At the restaurant, tap **Order food (scan table QR)**.
2. Point the camera at the **QR code on your table**.
3. Browse the **Menu** by category. Check **allergens**.
4. Add items to your cart. Add **Allergies or special instructions** if needed.
5. Tap **Review order**, then **Submit order**. Nothing is sent until you submit.
6. Pick **Pay at the table / outlet** or **Charge to my room** (when checked in).
7. Follow **Your order** to see progress and "Ready in about N min".

### 2.7 Chat with the hotel
- Tap **How can we help?** (or **Chat with the hotel**).
- Type in **Type a message** and send. The front desk answers in the same thread.

### 2.8 Loyalty points and rewards
- Open **Loyalty** to see your **points**, your **tier** and how many points to the **next tier**.
- You earn points for every ₱100 you spend (the rate is shown on screen).
- Tap **Redeem** on a reward. Points apply to a confirmed or current stay ("Applied to your stay").

### 2.9 Leave feedback
After your stay, open the booking, tap **Feedback**, rate **How was your stay?**, add a comment (optional) and tap **Send**.

### 2.10 Profile
Open **Profile** for:
- **Personal details**
- **Points and tier**
- **Stays**
- **Services used**
- **Payment history**
- **Language** and **Sign out**

## 3. Staff guide

Staff work in the **Staff area**. On web and tablets there is a side menu; on a phone it is a menu you open from the top.

| Menu item | Use it for |
|-----------|------------|
| **Front desk** | Today's overview: arrivals, departures, in-house guests, open tasks |
| **Reservations** | The booking queue and each booking's actions |
| **Tasks** | Service requests from guests |
| **Restaurant** | Food orders |
| **Chat** | Guest messages |
| **Leave requests** | Your own leave |

### 3.1 Front desk
- See **Arrivals**, **Departures**, **In-house** and **Open tasks** at a glance.
- Use the search box to find a guest by name, email, phone or booking code.

### 3.2 Handle reservations
1. Open **Reservations** and pick a booking.
2. Depending on its status you can:
   - **Approve** or **Decline** a pending booking (when the hotel requires approval).
   - **Check in** the guest when they arrive.
   - **Check out** the guest when they leave.
   - **Mark no-show** if the guest never arrives.
   - **Re-issue Wi-Fi** if the guest needs new Wi-Fi details.

### 3.3 Service tasks
1. Open **Tasks**. Use the filters (**Mine**, **My department**, **All**).
2. Tap a task and **Accept** it (or **Escalate**).
3. Work it step by step. Each status you set shows on the guest's timeline.
4. For quote-based services, tap **Send quote**.
5. Use **Decline** with a **Reason** if it cannot be done.

### 3.4 Restaurant orders
1. Open **Restaurant**. New orders appear in a queue.
2. Move each order forward with the status button (for example accepted → preparing → ready).
3. Use **Cancel order** if needed.

### 3.5 Chat inbox
1. Open **Chat** and tap a guest thread.
2. Read and reply in **Type a message**.

### 3.6 Leave requests
Open **Leave requests** → **Apply for leave**, choose **Dates** and **Reason**, then submit. You can see your leave under **My leave**.

## 4. Admin guide

Admins can do everything Staff can (under **Front desk**), plus set up and manage the hotel.

| Menu item | Use it for |
|-----------|------------|
| **Admin** (home) | Dashboard: today's numbers, alerts |
| **Front desk** | Same tools as Staff |
| **Rooms & rates** | Room types, rooms, rates, blocks |
| **Services** | Service catalog and request forms |
| **Restaurant** | Outlets, menus, tables, QR codes |
| **Payments & refunds** | Money in and out |
| **Reports** | Occupancy and revenue |
| **Staff** | People, roles, leave |
| **Loyalty points** | Points rules and rewards |
| **Guides & promotions** | Hotel guide and promotion content |
| **Hotel settings** | Name, address, map, policies |
| **Audit log** | Who changed what |

### 4.1 Dashboard
Shows **Occupancy tonight**, **Collected today**, **Refunded today**, **Net today**, and **Alerts** such as **Failed notifications** and **Failed refunds**. Check alerts every day.

### 4.2 Hotel settings
1. Open **Hotel settings**.
2. Fill in **Hotel name**, address, map location, check-in and check-out times.
3. Set money rules: **Deposit %**, **Free cancellation (hours before check-in)** and **Refund after free period %**.
4. Choose **Staff approve each paid booking** if you want to approve every booking.
5. Set **Wi-Fi mode** (portal or psk) and the shared password for psk.
6. Tap **Save**.

### 4.3 Rooms and rates
1. Open **Rooms & rates** → **Add room** (a room type), fill in **Name**, **Max guests**, **Base price**, **Amenities** and **Photos**, then **Save**.
2. Add the individual rooms (room numbers) under the type.
3. **Rates → Set price for dates** changes the price for a date range. Leave the price **empty** to go back to the base price.
4. **Room blocks** take a room out of sale (for maintenance, for example). Use **Unblock** to put it back.

### 4.4 Services
1. Open **Services** → add a service.
2. Set **Title**, **Description**, **Category**, **Department** and **Price model** (fixed, per item, per person, per night or **Price on request**).
3. Under **Guest form fields**, use **Add field** to choose what guests must fill in.
4. Choose **Staff accepts each request** or **Accept automatically**.
5. **Save draft**, check the list under **Missing before publishing**, then **Publish**. Use **Pause** to stop it temporarily.

### 4.5 Restaurant
1. Open **Restaurant** → **Add outlet**.
2. Add **Tables**. Each table gets a **QR code**. Print it and place it on the table.
3. **New QR code** replaces a table's code. **The old code stops working.**
4. Open the outlet's **Menu** to add categories and items with price, **allergens**, photo and **Order ahead (minutes)** timing.

### 4.6 Payments and refunds
- See every payment and its status.
- Issue a **Refund** when needed.
- Check **Failed refunds** and retry.

### 4.7 Reports
Open **Reports**, choose **Occupancy** or **Revenue**, set the dates, then **Copy as CSV** to paste into a spreadsheet.

### 4.8 Staff and roles
1. Open **Staff** → **Add staff**.
2. Enter **Name**, **Email**, **Department**, **Role** and **Permissions**. A **Temporary password** is given for the first sign-in.
3. **Remove staff access** takes their access away.
4. Review and approve **leave** here.

### 4.9 Loyalty
Set **Points per ₱100**, define tiers and rewards (with their **points cost**), and use **Adjust points** (+ add, − remove) with a reason when fixing a guest's balance.

### 4.10 Guides and promotions
Create content for the **Hotel guide** and promotions. **Save draft** while writing, then **Publish**.

### 4.11 Audit log
Shows **Date**, **Actor**, **Action** and **Entity** for every important change. Use it to answer "who changed this?"

## 5. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| "Can't reach the server. Check your connection." | Check your internet and tap **Try again**. On web, ask your administrator to allow the website. |
| Sign-in does not work on web | Use **HTTPS** (or localhost while testing). Plain HTTP on another address cannot keep a session. |
| Code did not arrive | Wait a minute and tap **Send code** again. The message is the same whether or not the account exists. |
| "Your hold expired" | Search again and re-select the room. |
| Paid but page still says "Waiting for payment" | Keep the page open: it updates by itself. If the booking is released, any charge is refunded. |
| Cannot redeem points | Points only apply to a confirmed or current stay. |
| Guest signed in on web sees "use the mobile app" | Expected. Guests use the iOS or Android app. |
