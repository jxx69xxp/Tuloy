# Budget Davao inn anchors realistic demo seed

D' Morvie Suites is a **small budget inn brand in Mindanao, not a resort or a full-service hotel**, and very little about it is published. The likely main branch is at **224 San Pedro St, Poblacion, Davao City**, with **20 rooms**. A second branch, D' Morvie Suites Lapasan in Cagayan de Oro, has 19 rooms ([Trip.com Davao](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao); [Trip.com Lapasan](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/cagayan-de-oro-hotel-detail-26440535/d-morvie-suites-lapasan)). The firm facts are thin. There are two non-smoking room types (Standard Single and Double), check-in at 14:00 and check-out at 12:00, a **fixed PHP 200 deposit**, no pets, and free parking. Wi-Fi exists but guests call it weak. The airport (DVO) is roughly **8 to 11 km away, about 29 minutes by car**. There is **no published PHP room rate, no restaurant, no confirmed services, and no promotions or loyalty program**. The real profile is a cheap downtown inn that sells both overnights and short stays. So a demo that wants dining, services and promos must borrow prices from comparable Davao hotels or invent them, and label them that way. This report keeps three kinds of content apart: sourced facts with confidence levels, comparable-market figures, and invented suggestions. It ends with a ready seed outline for a fictional hotel, "Lunhaw Suites Davao".

**How to read the labels.** **[H]** means stated directly on a fetched listing or official page. **[M]** means from an aggregator, a review summary, or sources that conflict. **[L]** means a search snippet, an undated source, or something the fetched page contradicts. **Comparable** means a real price from another Davao business, not D' Morvie. **Invented** means a plausible value with no source. A note on sources: no official website was found, and the Facebook page could not be fetched. Most facts come from Trip.com and from Wanderlog's summary of about 1,231 Google reviews ([Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites)).

## Two Mindanao branches sell cheap, short, air-conditioned stays

The Davao branch is the better template. It has far more online presence: a **3.4/5 Google rating from about 1,231 reviews**. Wanderlog calls it a "budget-friendly traveler's inn" and says it is good value for **stays under about six hours** ([Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites)). Trip.com labels both branches **3-star** [M]. That label looks auto-assigned, and no DOT accreditation record was found. The reviews describe a 1 to 2-star experience, so seed the fictional hotel as a budget inn ([Trip.com Davao](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)). Both Trip.com listings share one business phone number, which suggests a single operator [M]. The phone numbers are deliberately left out of this report and should not appear in seed data.

The two confirmed room types are the **Standard Single Room (1 single bed)** and the **Double Room (1 double bed)**, both non-smoking [H] ([Trip.com AE](https://ae.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)). Lapasan reviews mention a "Double Bed room" booked as a 12-hour stay [M], and one snippet mentions a "Junior Room" [L]. Guest reviews list the room features as **air conditioning, a private bathroom with shower and bidet, a TV with Netflix, and an elevator** [M] ([Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites)). No room sizes, occupancy limits or views were published anywhere. Trip.com showed **no PHP prices** for either branch in October 2026 [H for absence]. A Kayak snippet showing "38 SAR+" turned out to belong to a different Davao listing, so it was excluded.

Rates are therefore the biggest invented area. The research team's estimate for a Davao or Cagayan de Oro budget inn is **PHP 900 to 1,800 per night** and **PHP 300 to 600 for short-stay blocks**. This is **invented, not sourced**. The 3-, 6- and 12-hour blocks match how guests actually use the property, as described above.

## No restaurant, pool or service menu exists to copy

The research found **no restaurant, café, bar, room service, laundry, transfer or tour service** at either branch. The Trip.com FAQ says the Davao branch **"does not provide breakfast"** [H] ([Trip.com AE](https://ae.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)). A snippet claiming paid breakfast from 6 AM to 1 PM is contradicted by the fetched page and looks like generic template text [L]. A pool is hinted at in one review summary but is not confirmed by any listing [L]. One review complains about party noise from "downstairs" until about 3 AM. That hints at a ground-floor venue, but it is unconfirmed [L] ([Trip.com Davao](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)). The only service the reviews support is that **staff allow stay extensions** [M] ([Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites)). That is a real hook for a late check-out or extension service.

Comparable Davao prices give the invented catalog a realistic scale. **Hotel breakfast buffets** cost about **PHP 699** at Waterfront Insular ([Travelocity](https://www.travelocity.com/es/Davao-Hoteles-Waterfront-Insular-Hotel-Davao.h1330474.Opiniones-Hotel)), about **PHP 900** at Seda Abreeza ([lastminute.co.nz](https://lastminute.co.nz/Mati-Hotels-Seda-Abreeza.h6024084.Hotel-Information)), and **PHP 1,100 per adult** at dusitD2 ([lastminute.co.nz](https://lastminute.co.nz/Davao-Hotels-DusitD2-Davao.h32618529.Hotel-Information)). A budget inn sits well below this. At a Davao food hub, silog plates (cured meat or tocino with garlic rice and egg) start at **PHP 160**, with extra rice from **PHP 20** ([foodpanda](https://www.foodpanda.ph/restaurant/szqp/lalanda-food-hub)). For transport, a metered **airport taxi to downtown costs about PHP 100 to 185** ([Transfeero](https://www.transfeero.com/en/davao-francisco-bangoy-international-airport-transfers-dvo/)). A **private sedan transfer costs PHP 800 to 1,200, and a van PHP 1,500 to 2,500** ([Uncharted Lens](https://unchartedlens.com/routes/philippines/davao-airport-dvo-to-jp-laurel-avenue-hotel-district/)). **Self-drive car rental** runs from about **PHP 1,881 per day** ([Cheapflights PH](https://www.cheapflights.com.ph/car-rentals/Davao/?sck=SEM)). A no-show at the dusitD2 hotel spa is charged **PHP 1,500 per hour**, which implies spa rates of PHP 1,500 or more ([Dusit Namm Spa](https://www.dusit.com/dusitd2-davao/specialoffers/namm-spa)). A private Samal Island boat tour costs about **PHP 9,500 per boat**, but that figure is undated [L] ([GMA](https://gmanetwork.com/news/newstv/biyahenidrew/342724/samal-island-the-underrated-paradise-a-biyahe-ni-drew-itinerary/story)).

No Davao-specific source was found for **laundry per-kilo rates, scooter rental, a car with driver, in-room massage, extra beds or room-service surcharges**. Every figure for these in the seed outline is invented. Any café or outlet in the demo is also fictional by definition. It can still feel authentically Davao by using silog breakfasts, grilled tuna panga, and durian or mango shakes.

## Poblacion location puts markets, parks and 911 within reach

The Davao address sits near **7.0684 N, 125.6039 E** [M], in downtown Poblacion beside Bankerohan Market ([Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites)). Trip.com's map gives walking distances [H]: **St. Jude Thaddeus Parish 0.28 km, Bankerohan Public Market 0.37 km, Davao Doctors College 0.42 km, the Philippine Eagle Sculpture 0.53 km, and People's Park 0.54 km** ([Trip.com map](https://www.trip.com/hot/hotel-map/d%27-morvie-suites---davao-map.html)). Gaisano Citimall is listed at 0.27 km [M]. San Pedro Cathedral is about 1.4 km away, from a summary rather than a fetched page [M] ([Trip.com AE](https://ae.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)). Trip.com gives the **airport distance inconsistently, as 8 km, 8.3 km and 10.9 km**, with a drive of about 29 minutes. A range of "about 9 to 11 km, 25 to 40 minutes" is the honest version.

Several attractions make good guide content [H] ([Grab PH](https://www.grab.com/ph/attractions/davao-city/)). Downtown, there is **San Pedro Cathedral** (the city's oldest church, 1847), **People's Park** with its durian-shaped dome, **Museo Dabawenyo**, and the **Roxas Night Market** for evening food. Further out, there is the **Philippine Eagle Center**, **Eden Nature Park** (about an hour's drive), **Malagos Garden Resort**, **Crocodile Park**, **Jack's Ridge**, and **D' Bone Collector Museum**. **Samal Island** is a ferry ride away. From Sasa Wharf it takes about 10 minutes for about PHP 10 to 15. From Sta. Ana Wharf it takes about 45 minutes for about PHP 60 [M] ([PhilippineBeaches](https://www.philippinebeaches.org/?p=12203)). Check current schedules before using them.

The city rules are the most reliably sourced guide items. **Dial 911 (Davao City Central 911)**, which has worked on all networks since May 2025 [H] ([Davao City](https://davaocity.gov.ph/departments/social-services/central-911/)). The **anti-smoking ordinance** fines PHP 1,000, then 2,000, then 5,000 plus jail. The **Liquor Code** bans alcohol sales from 1 to 8 AM and, as of 2026, bans public drinking at all hours [H] ([Davao City VRU](https://davaocity.gov.ph/peace-and-order/vru-reminds-public-anew-to-follow-anti-smoking-liquor-ban-ordinances/); [SunStar](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/davao/nearly-4000-anti-smoking-liquor-code-violations-cited-in-h1-2026)). Emergency 911 is a public number, so it is safe to seed. Leave out every other real phone number.

## Promotions are absent, so Kadayawan and guest complaints shape them

D' Morvie has **no promotions, packages or loyalty program** that could be found. The only deals are platform-level ones, such as Trip.com's "We Price Match" and Trip Coins [M] ([Trip.com Davao](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)). Any promo in the demo is therefore invented. The strongest real-world hook is **Kadayawan**. In 2026 it ran from **August 7 to 31**, with the Grand Weekend on August 14 to 16, and the city targeted about **250,000 tourists** [H] ([SunStar](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/davao/kadayawan-2026-pamulak-indak-indak-set-on-august-16); [Manila Bulletin](https://mb.com.ph/2026/07/22/davao-city-eyes-250000-tourists-for-kadayawan)).

Guest sentiment points to the operations the app should demo. Guests **praise the low price, air-conditioning, private bathrooms, Netflix and flexible extensions**. They **complain about mosquitoes, a chlorine smell, untidy towels, faulty toilets, noise until about 3 AM, and weak Wi-Fi** [M] ([Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites); [Trip.com Davao](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)). Each complaint maps to a demo feature: a housekeeping readiness checklist, a pest-control task, quiet-hours notices, and a Wi-Fi help guide item. That makes the demo feel grounded without copying anything the real property offers.

## Conclusion

The research shows that the brand behind the name matters less than its segment. D' Morvie offers a realistic **template for a Philippine short-stay city inn**: tiny room inventory, hourly blocks, a token deposit, no F&B, and familiar upkeep complaints. A seed set drawn from it will look more authentic than a generic 4-star demo. Almost every number that makes a demo feel full, though, including rates, menus, services and promos, has no D' Morvie source. Those values should stay clearly fictional, and the brand name and contact details should never be reused.

For Tuloy specifically, the most valuable borrowing is not the price list. It is the **operational texture**: short-stay pricing, extension requests, deposit refunds, pest and noise tasks, and Wi-Fi help. These exercise the staff and admin flows that a polished resort seed would never touch. The main open risk is that the real rate card and any promos probably live only on the unfetched Facebook page. That is a further reason to treat every rate here as a fictional placeholder rather than a market benchmark.
