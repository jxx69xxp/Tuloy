# D' Morvie Suites (Philippines): Dining Outlets and Guest Services

Research date: 2026-10-13. Confidence levels: High (H), Medium (M), Low (L). No personal data on staff or guests was collected; review content is summarized only as it relates to facilities.

## Which property is "D Morvie"?

### Takeaway
"D Morvie" is **D' Morvie Suites**, a small budget inn brand with two listed branches, both in Mindanao. The **Davao City branch (224 San Pedro St, Poblacion)** is the likeliest target: it has about 1,231 Google reviews, while the Cagayan de Oro branch has only a few reviews on OTAs. Both are tiny short-stay inns with about 20 rooms each, not full-service hotels.

### Cited Findings
- **D' Morvie Suites – Davao:** 224 San Pedro St, Poblacion District, Davao City, Davao del Sur; 20 rooms; listed as "3-star" on Trip.com. Room types are a Standard Single (1 single bed) and a Double (1 double bed), both non-smoking (H) — [Trip.com PH](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)
- **D' Morvie Suites Lapasan:** corner of Lapasan Highway and Yacapin St, Cagayan de Oro, Misamis Oriental; 19 rooms (H) — [Trip.com PH](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/cagayan-de-oro-hotel-detail-26440535/d-morvie-suites-lapasan). It is within a 10-minute walk of Limketkai Center and SM CDO Downtown Premier (M, from a search summary) — [Trip.com map](https://www.trip.com/hot/hotel-map/d%27-morvie-suites-lapasan-map.html)
- Wanderlog lists the Davao branch as an **inn** with 3.4/5 from 1,231 Google reviews. It describes the place as a low-cost option often used for **short stays of under about six hours**, with an elevator, and says it often fills up at night (M; aggregated, date not shown) — [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites)
- Both branches share the same phone number on Trip.com, which suggests one operator (M) — [Trip.com Davao](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao); [Trip.com Lapasan](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/cagayan-de-oro-hotel-detail-26440535/d-morvie-suites-lapasan)
- Wanderlog says the Davao branch's website is a Facebook page ("D-Morvie-Suites"). I could not retrieve that page (L) — [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites)

### Inferences

## Guest services and prices

### Takeaway
The only confirmed services are **free on-site parking** (though Wanderlog conflicts on this), a **PHP 200 fixed deposit**, check-in from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00, an elevator, air conditioning, TV with Netflix, and Wi-Fi of variable quality. There is **no evidence of a pool, gym, spa, laundry, transfers, tours or rentals**. For a demo catalog, use the Davao comparables below.

### Cited Findings (property)
- Check-in from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00 (H). A **PHP 200 fixed deposit** is required (H). Pets and service animals are not allowed (H). For extra beds or children, guests must contact the hotel (H) — [Trip.com PH Davao](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)
- Lapasan has the same 14:00/12:00 times and PHP 200 deposit (H). A Jan 2025 review describes a dispute over a partial (PHP 100) refund of that deposit (M) — [Trip.com Lapasan](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/cagayan-de-oro-hotel-detail-26440535/d-morvie-suites-lapasan). HotelPlanner gives check-in as 1:00 PM, which conflicts (L) — [HotelPlanner](https://www.hotelplanner.com/Hotels/12765-in-Cagayan-de-Oro-Philippines.html)
- Parking: Trip.com says free on site (M). Wanderlog says there is no dedicated space, though a review mentions wide parking. **Conflicting** — [Trip.com](https://ae.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao); [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites)
