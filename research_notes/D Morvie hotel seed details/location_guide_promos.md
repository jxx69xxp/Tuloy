# D' Morvie Suites (Philippines): Location, Getting There, Landmarks, Promotions and Hotel Guide

Research date: 2026-10-13. Confidence tags: [H] high, [M] medium, [L] low. Only business-level contact info is included. No staff or guest personal data was collected.

## 1. Which property is "D Morvie"?

### Takeaway
Two budget properties share the name "D' Morvie Suites": one in Davao City (Poblacion) and one in Cagayan de Oro (Lapasan). The Davao one is almost certainly the main or intended property. It has about 1,231 Google reviews and a Facebook page. The CDO branch has only a handful of OTA reviews.

### Cited Findings
- [H] D' Morvie Suites – Davao: 224 San Pedro St, Poblacion District, Davao City, Davao del Sur. Listed as 3-star on Trip.com, with 20 rooms (Standard Single and Double, all non-smoking) — [Trip.com Davao](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)
- [H] D' Morvie Suites Lapasan: corner of Lapasan Highway and Yacapin Street, Cagayan de Oro, Misamis Oriental. 19 rooms — [Trip.com Lapasan](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/cagayan-de-oro-hotel-detail-26440535/d-morvie-suites-lapasan)
- [M] The Lapasan branch is said to be within a 10-minute walk of Limketkai Center and SM CDO Downtown Premier. It is about 26 km from Laguindingan Airport, and its front desk keeps limited hours — [Trip.com Lapasan](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/cagayan-de-oro-hotel-detail-26440535/d-morvie-suites-lapasan) (walk time from a search-result summary of Trip.com listings)
- [M] Wanderlog calls the Davao property a "budget traveler's inn" with a 3.4/5 rating from 1,231 Google reviews. It links a Facebook page (facebook.com/pages/D-Morvie-Suites/354107161378192) — [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites)
- [M] Both Trip.com listings give the same phone number (+63-925-500-3602). Wanderlog gives a different business number (+63 943 388 1153). This suggests a single operator, but the numbers conflict — [Trip.com Davao](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao); [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites)

### Inferences
- Seed data should use the Davao (Poblacion) property as the primary hotel. The CDO Lapasan branch could be a second "branch" in a multi-property demo.
- Both share Trip.com policies (check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00, PHP 200 deposit, no pets). This points to one small local chain.

## 2. Address, coordinates, airport/seaport, transport (Davao property)

### Takeaway
The hotel is in downtown Poblacion near Bankerohan Market, at about 7.0684 N, 125.6039 E. It is about 8–11 km (20–40 min by car) from Francisco Bangoy International Airport (DVO). The Samal Island ferries leave from Sasa and Sta. Ana wharves. Grab, metered taxis and jeepneys are the main ways to get around.

### Cited Findings
- [H] Address: 224 San Pedro St, Poblacion District, Davao City 8000, Davao del Sur — [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites); [Trip.com](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)
- [M] Coordinates 7.068409, 125.603942, taken from the Wanderlog map pin — [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites)
- [M] Airport distance is given as about 8 km in the property description, about 8.3 km on the Trip.com map page, and 10.9 km (about 29 min by car) in the Trip.com FAQ — [Trip.com](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao); [Trip.com map](https://www.trip.com/hot/hotel-map/d%27-morvie-suites---davao-map.html)
- [L] Airport to city center: Grab about PHP 80–250, though one guide says PHP 250–500. Metered taxi about PHP 100–200 at arrivals. Jeepney about PHP 10–15 from the main road, but impractical with luggage. Off-peak 15–40 min; rush hours (7–9 AM, 5–7 PM) add delays. These are 2026 aggregator or commercial pages, and their figures conflict — [UnchartedLens](https://unchartedlens.com/routes/philippines/davao-airport-dvo-to-city-center-san-pedro-street/); [PhilippinesEmbassyInfo DVO guide 2026](https://philippinesembassyinfo.com/airports/davao-francisco-bangoy); [Transfeero](https://www.transfeero.com/en/davao-francisco-bangoy-international-airport-transfers-dvo/)
- [M] Seaports and Samal ferry: Sasa Wharf has frequent short crossings (about 10 min, about PHP 10–15). Sta. Ana Wharf boats take about 45 min for about PHP 60. Each wharf takes two jeepney rides from downtown (about PHP 15–20). Older sources say ferries run 05:30–23:30, with the operator announcing 24-hour service in Dec 2021. Verify current schedules — [PhilippineBeaches](https://www.philippinebeaches.org/?p=12203); [Noonsite](https://www.noonsite.com/place/philippines/mindanao-southern-region/davao/view/transport-and-travel); [Wikipedia Port of Davao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Davao)

Wanderlog's About section says there is no dedicated parking, but reviewers mention wide parking spaces — [Trip.com](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao); [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1793044/dmorvie-suites)

### Inferences
- A sensible "Getting here" line: from DVO airport, take Grab or a metered taxi about 9–11 km south-west to San Pedro St, near Bankerohan Market (about 25–40 min). For Samal Island, take Grab or a taxi to Sasa Wharf.
- Tricycles are mostly limited to side streets and barangays in Davao. Downtown travel is mainly by jeepney, taxi and Grab. This is general local knowledge with no source in this research.

### Gaps
- No authoritative 2025–2026 fare matrix for jeepneys or taxis was found (the LTFRB fare matrix was not checked).
- Exact distances from the hotel to Sasa and Sta. Ana wharves were not found.

## 3. Nearby landmarks and things a hotel guide would recommend

### Takeaway
The hotel is within walking distance of Bankerohan Market, St. Jude Thaddeus Parish, People's Park and the Philippine Eagle sculpture. San Pedro Cathedral, Museo Dabawenyo, D' Bone Collector Museum and Roxas Night Market are a short ride away. Day trips include the Philippine Eagle Center, Eden Nature Park, Malagos Garden Resort, Crocodile Park, Jack's Ridge and Samal Island.

### Cited Findings
- [H] Walking distances from the Trip.com map: Mallengke 0.19 km, St. Jude Thaddeus Parish 0.28 km, Bankerohan Public Market 0.37 km, Davao Doctors College 0.42 km, Philippine Eagle Sculpture 0.53 km, People's Park 0.54 km — [Trip.com map](https://www.trip.com/hot/hotel-map/d%27-morvie-suites---davao-map.html)
- [M] Other short distances from the Trip.com listing: Masjid Bankerohan 0.32 km, Gaisano Citimall (kids playroom) 0.27 km — [Trip.com](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)
- [M] San Pedro Cathedral is about 1.4 km (0.9 mi) away. This figure comes from an OTA summary in search results, not a fetched page — [Trip.com AE](https://ae.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)
- [M] OTA blurbs place the hotel within a short drive of SM City Davao (Ecoland) and Davao Doctors Hospital — search-result summary of [Trip.com](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)
- [H] San Pedro Cathedral is the city's oldest church (1847) and is downtown. People's Park has a durian-shaped dome and Kublai Millan sculptures. Museo Dabawenyo covers indigenous and colonial history. Roxas Night Market is an evening food and thrift market on Roxas Ave — [Grab PH attractions](https://www.grab.com/ph/attractions/davao-city/)
- [H] Further out: Philippine Eagle Center in Malagos (conservation center with guided tours). Eden Nature Park on Mt. Talomo (80 ha, Skycycle, zipline, horseback riding, about 1 hour's drive). Malagos Garden Resort (butterfly sanctuary, cacao farm, chocolate museum). Crocodile Park (shows and feedings). Jack's Ridge (WWII HQ turned dining hill). D' Bone Collector Museum (700+ skeletons) — [Grab PH attractions](https://www.grab.com/ph/attractions/davao-city/)
- [M] Suggested half-day walking route: Bankerohan Market in the morning, then Museo Dabawenyo, San Pedro Cathedral, People's Park, and Roxas Night Market from about 6 PM (open until about midnight) — [Traveloka itinerary](https://www.traveloka.com/en-ph/explore/destination/davao-city-half-day-tour-itinerary/1003569); [SunStar 21 things](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/more-articles/21-things-to-do-in-davao)

- Map pins for seed data could include Bankerohan Market, People's Park, San Pedro Cathedral, Roxas Night Market, Gaisano Mall area, SM Ecoland, Sasa Wharf and DVO airport.
- Good food tips for the guide: durian and pomelo at Bankerohan, and street food at Roxas Night Market.

## 4. Promotions, loyalty, seasonal events, guest sentiment

### Takeaway
No promotions, packages or loyalty program could be found for D' Morvie. The only "deals" are OTA-level ones: Trip.com's "We Price Match" and Trip Coins. Kadayawan in August is the main seasonal event to build promos around. Guests praise the price and location. Complaints are mostly about cleanliness, mosquitoes, noise, weak Wi-Fi and Netflix, and service.

Lapasan also lists no promos — [Trip.com Davao](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao); [Trip.com Lapasan](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/cagayan-de-oro-hotel-detail-26440535/d-morvie-suites-lapasan)

## 5. Hotel guide content (house rules, emergency info, local tips)

### Takeaway
Use the known OTA policies for house rules: check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00, PHP 200 deposit, no pets, non-smoking rooms. Add Davao's well-known city ordinances (strict anti-smoking and liquor rules) and Central 911 as the local emergency number.

### Cited Findings
- [H] Check-in from 14:00, check-out by 12:00, a fixed PHP 200 deposit, pets and service animals not allowed, non-smoking rooms, children and extra beds by request — [Trip.com](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/davao-hotel-detail-26439024/d-morvie-suites-davao)
- [M] 24-hour front desk and elevator at the Davao property — search-result summary of OTA listings
- [H] Emergency: dial 911 (Davao City Central 911), reachable on all networks since May 2025. Non-emergency line (082) 296-9626 — [Davao City Central 911](https://davaocity.gov.ph/departments/social-services/central-911/); [SunStar](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/davao/central-911-opens-access-to-all-telcos)
- [H] Anti-smoking ordinance: no smoking in public places or public transport; fines PHP 1,000, then 2,000, then 5,000 plus jail. Smoke only in designated areas — [Davao City VRU](https://davaocity.gov.ph/peace-and-order/vru-reminds-public-anew-to-follow-anti-smoking-liquor-ban-ordinances/)
- [H] Liquor code: no sale or drinking of alcohol from 1–8 AM. As of 2026, public drinking is banned at all hours under the consolidated Liquor Code. Fines PHP 3,000, then 5,000, then 5,000 plus jail. The rules apply to tourists too — [Davao City VRU](https://davaocity.gov.ph/peace-and-order/vru-reminds-public-anew-to-follow-anti-smoking-liquor-ban-ordinances/); [SunStar H1 2026](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/davao/nearly-4000-anti-smoking-liquor-code-violations-cited-in-h1-2026)
- [M] Unattended bags in public places can be penalized. Security checks are common; tourists are urged to follow the city's "culture of security" — [SunStar](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/davao/feature/5-ways-to-enjoy-davao-city); [Davao City](https://davaocity.gov.ph/peace-and-order/dabawenyos-tourists-urged-to-uphold-culture-of-security/)
- [M] Expect road closures around the Kadayawan weekend — [Davao City (via search)](https://www.davaocity.gov.ph/?p=20559)

### Inferences
- Guide sections could cover: Wi-Fi and TV help (Wi-Fi and Netflix are a common complaint), quiet hours (because of noise complaints), mosquito repellent available at the front desk, a reminder about the city smoking and liquor rules, 911, and the nearest hospital (Davao Doctors Hospital is a short drive per OTA blurbs).
- Durian is commonly banned inside hotel rooms in the Philippines. This is a typical house rule but was not sourced for this property.

### Gaps
- The property's own house rules (quiet hours, visitor policy, durian policy, curfew) were not found.
- The nearest hospital distance was not verified.
